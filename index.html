Tidvori Privacy Policy

Updated 30 October 2026 · OrbickK

Who operates Tidvori

Tidvori (com.orbickk.tidvori) is a household cleaning planner provided by OrbickK / Orbickk_Lab. Contact orbickk@gmail.com for privacy or support questions. It is intended for adults managing their household.

Chores stay on your device

Chore names, rooms, schedules, completion history, session progress and settings are stored in encrypted app-private storage on your Android device. We do not run a server that receives these records, and do not send chore text to Google Play Billing. No app account, advertising SDK or developer behavior analytics is included. The app retains up to 200 chores and the latest 5,000 completions.

Google Play purchases and technical diagnostics

Google Play processes the optional one-time Pro purchase under Google's terms and privacy policy. Tidvori handles the product, signed purchase evidence, token and purchase state to verify and restore access. We do not receive payment card details or your Google password. The included Google Play Billing library and its transport components send technical diagnostics to Google, including API outcomes and latency, app/SDK versions, device and operating-system metadata, network type, locale, and random client/session and request identifiers. This can occur even when you do not buy Pro. These identifiers are not an advertising ID; chore names and history are not passed into this billing path. Google's retention and processing rules apply to its service records.

Complimentary access

An optional complimentary access code issued by Tidvori support unlocks the same Pro features without payment. The code is verified and stored encrypted only on this device, separately from chores and Play purchases. It is not sent to our server or Google. Local chore reset retains it; clearing app storage or uninstalling removes it. Re-enter a valid code to regain access.

Backups, notifications and permissions

Backup export and import use Android's file picker. Exported JSON files contain readable chore text and history and are not encrypted by Tidvori. You choose their location; a chosen cloud storage provider can process files under its own policy. Backups do not grant Pro access. Notification permission is requested only when you enable a reminder. Reminders are scheduled locally and may be delayed by Android. No contacts, camera, microphone or location access is requested.

Deletion and retention

Use Settings → Erase all local data, Android's clear-storage control, or uninstall to remove local chores, history and settings. Local signed purchase evidence is kept separately so resetting chores does not cancel Pro; clearing app storage or uninstalling removes that local cache, and Play can restore a valid purchase. Delete exported files separately. Local deletion does not cancel a Play purchase or delete Google's transaction and diagnostic records. Contact us to request help with information we control, such as support correspondence.

Website, support and changes

If you email us, your address and the information you choose to send are processed to answer your request. Avoid sending private household details unnecessarily. This policy website is static and has no added analytics or advertising scripts; the hosting provider processes network request information to serve and protect the site. We will update this page when practices change. Google policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. Hosting policy: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement.
